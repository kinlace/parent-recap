# Social Schools is read by signing in as its iOS app, with a stored password

Many schools in the Netherlands send parents their news through Social Schools, and a test family there finds that its email notifications don't replace logging in: some of what a school posts is only inside it (#904). Social Schools has no feed for parents and no published API, but its apps read everything as JSON from `https://api.socialschools.eu/api/v1/` with a bearer token, and its iOS app gets that token through OpenID Connect at `login.socialschools.eu`: an authorization request for the client `eu.socialschools.ios` with PKCE, the normal login form at `/Account/Login` (email, password and its request verification token), and a redirect to the app's own address `eu.socialschools.ios:/oauthredirect` with a code, which `/connect/token` exchanges for the token. That flow needs no browser: a program can follow the redirects itself and fill in the form (as HAChoresManager/ha-socialschools, MIT, does; not tried on an account here, since no one on the team has one). So the Social Schools Source signs in that way each evening, with the email in the config and the password in the system's secret store (ADR 0020, ADR 0030), as eVaka's is (ADR 0039), and reads `useraccounts/current` (the schools the account is a parent at, and at each the children, only their names and their groups' names), each school's `communityposts` back to `socialschools.lookback_hours`, and its `events` for the next four weeks. A post becomes a Message, told once, for the Kid whose group it was posted in, or the Household; an event becomes a calendar event the same way. The first slice is on a family's own computer, added from a terminal (`parent-recap setup socialschools`, the password typed at the secret store's own prompt); a step on the setup page and the hosted sign-up come later, once it has worked on a real account.

## Considered Options

- **A refresh token, kept in the secret store instead of the password, as the iOS app and ha-socialschools keep it.** Rejected for now: how long a refresh token lives, and whether each use replaces it, isn't known, and a lost one would need the family to type the password again anyway. Signing in fresh each evening needs nothing kept but what eVaka and Eepos keep, and the sign-in asks for no `offline_access`, so Social Schools makes no long-lived grant each evening. The access token lives only in the evening run's memory.
- **Signing in through a browser on the family's computer.** Rejected: an evening run has no one at the browser, and the web client's token lasts about an hour with no refresh token.
- **The email notifications alone, through the Gmail Source.** Rejected: they don't carry everything a school posts, which is why the family asked.
- **Reading the conversations inbox too.** Rejected for now: fetching a conversation's messages may mark them read at Social Schools, and Parent Recap never changes anything in a Source. The family's own conversations with teachers stay in the app.
- **Opening each post's own page (`communityposts/{id}`) for its full text.** Rejected: the feed already carries the whole body, and opening a post is what the web app does when a parent reads it, which may count as seen.

## Consequences

- The sign-in and the API are internal to Social Schools' apps and unversioned. When an answer isn't what they gave before, the Source says Social Schools "changed" on the Brief's coverage line, the rest of the Brief still goes out, and `doctor` says the same.
- Only the login form coming back after the email and password were sent is taken as a wrong password. An account that signs in only with Google, Microsoft or Apple has no Social Schools password, so it looks the same, and setup and `doctor` say so with it. A sign-in that leaves `login.socialschools.eu` or shows another page after the password (a code, a consent) is Unsupported: it is never followed, setup saves nothing and says why, and the Brief says the login failed. Such an account can't use this Source.
- The Source only sends GETs to the API, and only to `useraccounts/current`, `communityposts` and `events`; the client refuses any other path before it is sent. It never sends the "seen" statistics the web app sends to `statistics.socialschools.eu`, never opens a post's own page, never reads the inbox and never downloads an attachment: a post's attachments are named in its Message, and the file stays in Social Schools.
- Every post is to a group or the whole school, never to one family, so none is held back for its words (ADR 0013). Its text goes through the AI filter like any Source's: the teachers' and other children's names, phone numbers, addresses and links become placeholders.
- A post is a Kid's when one of its groups is one of that child's groups as `useraccounts/current` names them, compared without case or extra spaces, so a look-alike group never counts; a post in several Kids' groups, or in none, such as the whole school's news, is the Household's. A child is matched to a Kid by name, and setup keeps the match by the child's name in the config.
- Social Schools gives a whole day as midnight UTC to the midnight after its last day; the Source keeps those dates as written rather than moving them to the family's zone, which would make a day longer.
- The terms of service haven't been checked yet, and it hasn't been tried against a real account: the test family checks it once before it is offered to anyone else. Whether it works from the hosted server is unknown, so a Hosted Household can't have it, and the hosted Brief doesn't name it, as for Local Files.
