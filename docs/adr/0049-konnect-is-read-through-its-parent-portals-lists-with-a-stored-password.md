# Konnect is read through its parent portal's lists, with a stored password

A test family in the Netherlands has a child at an after-school care (BSO) whose parent app is Konnect's parent portal, which Dutch childcare providers such as Partou, Kibeo and Kappio run at `<provider>.ouderportaal.nl` (#905). Its messages, news and the days the child is booked don't reach the Brief. Konnect has no calendar feed and no published API, but the portal's web app reads everything as JSON: it signs in with `PUT /auth-api/login`, a JSON body `{"username", "password", "childMinder": false}`, and gets a token (`authToken`) that lasts 15 minutes, sent as `Authorization: Bearer` with every read under `/restservices-parent/`. A parent signs in with a username and password, with no CAPTCHA or second factor. This is taken from two open-source readers of the same portal, MatyasK/ha-ouderportaal (MIT, the same JSON sign-in) and anneschuth/konnect-cli (MIT, which signs in through a browser), not tried on an account: no one on the team has one. So the Konnect Source signs in as the portal's app does, with our own small reader on `requests`, and sends a browser's User-Agent, since some of the portal's addresses turn others away. Each provider is its own address with its own session, so a Household has one Konnect sign-in per provider, as it has one eVaka sign-in per city (ADR 0039). The password is kept in the system's secret store (ADR 0020, ADR 0030), as Eepos's and eVaka's are, and the rest of the sign-in, the provider's part of the address and the username, in the config. Of the children the portal lists, which come with their group, its phone number, an avatar and the birth date, only the ids and names are read.

The evening run signs in fresh each time, since the token outlives no run and the refresh cookie the portal also sets lasts an hour, then reads what the account may see (`parent/authorizations`, as the portal's app does at sign-in) and only lists:

- the Messages page's three lists, back to the lookback: the conversations (`logbook/overview`, each with its latest message in full), the news (`htmlnews/view`) and the newsletters and service messages (`newsletter`), the last two only as the start of their text the list gives. Each the daycare sent since the last run is a Message, a conversation for the Kid its children are; the family's own aren't. News and newsletters count as sent to everyone;
- the Agenda (`calendar/<from>/until/<to>`) of the next 14 days: each time a child is planned to be there, as a calendar event for the Kid the child is, titled in Dutch as the portal says it ("Opvang", with "optie" or "aangevraagd" for an option or a request not yet answered). An absence, and a request turned down, aren't events.

No single item is ever fetched: opening a message's or a news item's own page marks it read on the portal, and the family would lose the portal's own "new" marks. The timeline is never read: it holds photos of the children, often of other children too, and the day-rhythm entries (sleep, meals, medication, temperature), which are health data the Brief doesn't need. Nothing is written to the portal.

A portal that takes the password but wants a new one set first answers the sign-in with `mustResetPassword`. That is a sign-in that fails, said as a failed login on the Brief's coverage line, and `doctor` and setup say to sign in to the portal in a browser once, set the new password, then type it again. A sign-in is added only from a terminal, with `parent-recap setup konnect`, which checks it before anything is kept: the setup page has no Konnect step, so the Finnish families most Households are don't see one more step to skip, and the hosted sign-up has none either.

## Considered Options

- **A browser sign-in, as konnect-cli does.** Rejected: it needs a headless browser, Playwright and its Chromium, on every family's computer, for a sign-in that a JSON call does as well.
- **Refreshing the token through the evening, as ha-ouderportaal does.** Rejected: the run lasts a minute, well within one token, and a fresh sign-in each evening is less to keep and nothing to store.
- **Reading each message's or news item's own page for its whole text.** Rejected: it marks the item read on the portal. The conversations' list already has each latest message in full; for news and newsletters the start of their text is told, and the family reads the rest on the portal.
- **Reading the timeline, for the day reports.** Rejected: its photos are of other people's children too, and its day rhythm is health data. What a daycare wants a parent to act on comes as a message or news.
- **The password in the config.** Rejected: it is a password to an account with the Kids' personal data, so it gets the secret store's protection, as Eepos's and eVaka's do.

## Consequences

- The API is internal to the portal's own app and unversioned, and the terms of service aren't checked yet. When an answer isn't what the portal gave before, the Source says Konnect "changed" on the Brief's coverage line, the rest of the Brief still goes out, and `doctor` says the same. Messages that can't be read leave the planned times, and the line says Konnect was partly read.
- A wrong username or password is taken from the sign-in's HTTP 400, 401 or 403; any other failure is the portal being unreachable or having changed, so a changed sign-in never tells the family their password is wrong.
- A conversation's message is told once by the id of its latest message, so a reply from the daycare in the same conversation is told as a new message.
- The Agenda's parent meetings and activities aren't read yet: their shape wasn't in the references. Neither are a provider's closing days, other than as news.
- Untested against a real account when it was written: the test family checks it once.
- The hosted Brief's last line doesn't name Konnect among the Sources the hosted version doesn't read: a maintainer can add a sign-in with `setup konnect`.
